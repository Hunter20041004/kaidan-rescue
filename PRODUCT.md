# 怪談救援 — Product

## What it is
A single-file browser horror game in Traditional Chinese. A truck driver walks into a blacked-out Taiwanese farming village (山腳村) at night and brings trapped villagers back to the truck before the rooster crows, while monsters from Taiwanese and East-Asian folklore roam and "rule apparitions" impose taboos. The main mode, 七夜, is seven nights in a row ending on 頭七; a single quick night also exists.

## Who plays
- Taiwanese players aged 18–35 who share a link from LINE/IG. They usually open it on a phone first, then play properly on a laptop with headphones.
- They know the folklore (虎姑婆, 魔神仔, 紅衣小女孩, 頭七, 農民曆) and notice when it is wrong.

## What success feels like
- In play: fear and pressure. You hear things before you see them, and hiding is a gamble.
- Between nights: the weight of who you saved and who you lost.
- After a run: "one more run."

## Constraints
- One HTML file, no external assets except Google Fonts. Pixel-art world drawn on canvas; UI in HTML/CSS over it.
- Works at desktop 1280×800 and phone 375×812 with touch.
- Owner feedback that set the current direction (2026-10-28): the old UI was 「很大眾、很 AI」. It used generic East-Asian paper, seals and brush lettering on every screen.
